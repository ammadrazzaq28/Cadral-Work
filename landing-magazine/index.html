Most reminders are lost in the gap between thinking of something and finding the right app to write it down. Loopnote closes that gap. When a memo mentions a time (Friday, tomorrow morning, after school), it becomes a reminder with that time already set.

You say when. Loopnote puts it there.

Reminders sync with Apple Reminders and Calendar, so they show up alongside everything else you already plan. Nothing new to check and no second inbox. Smart reminders are part of Loopnote Pro.
